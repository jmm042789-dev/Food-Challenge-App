## 1. Gameplay Disclaimer

Fire Feast is a fictional, arcade-style game created for entertainment. Its gameplay, characters, restaurants, scores, prizes, rankings, rewards, and competitive presentation are fictional game mechanics.

Coins, XP, inventory, cosmetics, unlocks, and other virtual rewards exist only within the Game. They have no real-world cash value and cannot be exchanged for cash.

## 2. Health and Well-Being

We want Fire Feast to be an enjoyable experience. While playing:

- take regular breaks;
- avoid playing when you are overly tired;
- stop playing if you experience discomfort; and
- remain aware of your surroundings, especially while using touch gestures or playing in a public place.

Choose a comfortable posture and device position. Do not continue playing through pain, dizziness, eye strain, nausea, or other discomfort.

## 3. Photosensitive Players

Fire Feast may contain flashing lights, bright visual effects, animations, motion effects, and visual transitions.

If you have a history of photosensitive epilepsy or a similar condition, consider consulting a qualified healthcare professional before playing if you have concerns. Stop playing and seek medical advice if you experience symptoms such as dizziness, altered vision, eye or muscle twitching, loss of awareness, disorientation, involuntary movement, or a seizure.

This section provides general safety information and is not medical advice.

## 4. Motion and Visual Effects

Some Fire Feast gameplay and presentation effects may include:

- animated movement;
- screen shake;
- particle effects;
- camera movement; and
- bright visual effects.

Fire Feast includes **Reduced Motion** and **Camera Effects** controls in the in-game Settings screen. Reduced Motion minimizes supported animation and camera movement, while the Camera Effects control can disable supported arcade camera effects. These settings may not remove every visual change or transition.

Players may also use relevant accessibility or display settings provided by their device where available.

## 5. Fair Play
